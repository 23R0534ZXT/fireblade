# Support

Fireblade is maintained by one person and comes with no support commitment.

- **How do I run it?** The [README](README.md) and [deploy/host](deploy/host/README.md).
- **What does it do and not do?** [docs/host/STATUS.md](docs/host/STATUS.md).
- **Something is broken.** Open an [issue](https://github.com/23R0534ZXT/fireblade/issues/new/choose)
  with the version, what you did and what you saw. Do not attach evidence or client data.
- **A security problem.** [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), privately.
