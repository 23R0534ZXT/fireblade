# Support

Kagarasu is maintained by one person and comes with no support commitment.

- **How do I run it?** [Start a local instance](README.md#quick-start) or [deploy the per-case driver](deploy/host/README.md#one-container-per-case).
- **What does it do and not do?** [docs/host/STATUS.md](docs/host/STATUS.md).
- **Something is broken.** Open an [issue](https://github.com/23R0534ZXT/kagarasu/issues/new/choose)
  with the version, deployment mode, steps to reproduce and the observed result. Use synthetic or public data in examples and screenshots.
- **A security problem.** [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), privately.
